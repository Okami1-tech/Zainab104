TO MY BEAUTIFUL SARAUNIYA

My Hayati, 💜

Sometimes I think I don't tell you enough how much I admire the person you are.

And today, I don't just want to tell you that you're beautiful. I want to talk about the things that make you beautiful in ways that go far beyond what someone can see.

Your intelligence. I love that there's a mind behind your smile—a person who thinks, learns, understands, and has her own way of looking at the world. I admire that about you, Sarauniya. I want you to keep learning, keep growing, keep asking questions, and never make yourself smaller just to make other people comfortable.

Your thoughtfulness. You have ways of showing care that mean more than you may realize. Sometimes it isn't about doing something huge. It's about paying attention, encouraging someone, remembering what matters, or saying something at the right time. Those little things tell me something beautiful about your heart.

The way you understand people. I appreciate the effort you make to communicate, the moments you try to explain what you mean, and the times you show that you care about how someone feels. Nobody understands everything perfectly, and none of us gets everything right all the time. But the willingness to think, listen, and try means a lot to me.

Your own kind of brilliance. I hope you never measure your intelligence or your worth by comparing yourself to another person. You have your own strengths, your own perspective, your own potential, and your own things to contribute to this world. I hope you recognize them more and more as you grow.

And my love, I hope you know that you don't have to be perfect to be appreciated. You don't have to have the right words every time. You don't have to prove your intelligence to me, or keep doing things to earn my admiration.

I want to appreciate you as a whole person: your thoughts, your efforts, your good qualities, your growing edges, your serious moments, your playful moments, and all those little details that make you you.

One thing I really want for you is to believe in the woman you're becoming. Let your mind grow. Let your confidence grow. Protect your kindness, but remember that your voice matters too. Never be afraid to share your ideas, speak thoughtfully, or pursue the things that are important to you.

I'm proud of the things I see in you, Sarauniya. And I hope that, alongside the love I give you, you keep discovering reasons to be proud of yourself.

You are not special to me simply because you are my babe. I admire you because there are beautiful things about the person you are that deserve to be admired.

So if today is one of those days when you forget how much you have to offer, come back to this little letter. Read it slowly. Remember that someone sees more than your appearance. Someone notices your mind, your effort, your thoughtfulness, and the heart behind them.

That someone is me. ❤️

May Allah increase you in beneficial knowledge, wisdom, goodness, confidence, and beautiful character. May He protect your heart, bless your efforts, and help you become everything that is good for you in this life and the next.

And may I always remember to appreciate you properly—not just with sweet words, but through the way I listen to you, respect you, encourage you, and treat you.

You are appreciated, my queen. More than this little page could ever fully explain.

With love,
Muhammad 💜
